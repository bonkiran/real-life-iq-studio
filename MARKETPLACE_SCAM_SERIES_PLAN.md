# REAL-LIFE IQ Marketplace Scam Series Plan

Locked on 2026-09-24.

## Series structure

Total meaningful variations: 10
Total videos: 4

### Video 1: Marketplace Seller Scam: "I Already Paid You"
Variation 1: Fake payment screenshot or fake payment email.
Variation 2: Seller already shipped or handed over the item after fake payment proof.

Core solution:
Verify the money directly in your own bank or payment app before releasing, shipping, or handing over the item. Do not rely on screenshots, emails, or buyer messages as proof of payment.

### Video 2: Marketplace Buyer Scam: "Pay Me Outside Marketplace"
Variation 1: Seller asks buyer to pay outside Marketplace.
Variation 2: Expensive item offered at an unrealistically low price.
Variation 3: Seller claims Marketplace payment is not working and asks to move payment elsewhere.
Variation 4: Buyer already paid but item never arrives.

Core solution:
Stay within Marketplace protections when available, independently verify the seller and listing, and use protected payment methods.

### Video 3: Marketplace Overpayment / Refund Scam
Variation 1: Buyer claims they accidentally overpaid and asks the seller to send money back.
Variation 2: Buyer sends an overpayment by check and asks the seller to refund the difference.

Core solution:
Never return money based on an unverified payment. Verify the original payment directly with the bank or payment provider first.

### Video 4: Marketplace Verification Code Scam
Variation 1: Buyer asks seller for a six-digit verification code.
Variation 2: Seller already shared the code and needs to secure or recover the affected account.

Core solution:
Never share verification codes. If already shared, immediately secure the affected account.

## Production rule

Keep each video tightly scoped to its own scam type. Do not merge the four videos into one broad Marketplace video. Cross-reference another Marketplace episode instead of repeating full guidance.

## Current active work

Video 1, Variation 2: Seller already shipped or handed over the item after fake payment proof.

Locked decision point:
"The seller realizes the payment was fake only after the item is already gone. What can still be recovered?"

Current scope:
A seller has already shipped or handed over an item after trusting fake payment proof from a scammer posing as a buyer. The recovery path depends on whether the package is still in transit, already delivered, or the item was handed over in person.

Core recovery branches:
- Package still in transit: contact the carrier immediately and request any available intercept, return-to-sender, hold, or reroute option.
- Package already delivered: preserve tracking and delivery evidence, report the marketplace account, file fraud reports, and contact local law enforcement when appropriate.
- In-person handoff: do not confront the buyer; preserve profile/messages/location details and report the fraud.

Out of scope for this variation:
- Prevention before the item is released
- Overpayment and refund requests
- Verification-code scams
- Buyer-side off-platform payment scams
